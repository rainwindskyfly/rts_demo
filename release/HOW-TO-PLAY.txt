RTS Demo — Windows build
========================

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Extract this zip anywhere (e.g. Desktop).
2. Double-click RTSDemo.exe.
3. That's it — no Godot, no other files needed.

RTSDemo.exe and RTSDemo.pck must stay in the same folder.

CONTROLS
--------
Left click        select one unit
Left drag         box-select your own units
Right click       move / attack / harvest (depends on what you click)
W A S D / arrows  pan the camera   (Shift = faster)
Mouse wheel       zoom
S                 stop the selected units
H                 jump to your base
Space             jump to the latest fight
Ctrl+A            select all combat units
Ctrl+1..9         assign a control group
1..9              recall a control group
B                 toggle the build panel
1..5              pick a build-panel entry
Esc               cancel / pause menu

HOW TO WIN
----------
Destroy the enemy HQ (the big blue/red square). You also win on points when
the 20 minute timer runs out.

Your starting workers mine automatically. Right-click an ore field to send
them somewhere else, or right-click your HQ to unload them.

BUILD
-----
Barracks  120  produces Infantry and Tanks
Refinery  100  must be placed on an ore field; workers unload here
Infantry   50  cheap melee
Tank      150  armoured, long range
Worker     40  mines ore

Make a Barracks first, then mass infantry, then push.

ABOUT
-----
Built with Godot 4.7.2. All art is generated in code — the game ships with
zero image or audio files. Source: https://github.com/rainwindskyfly/rts_demo
